# NNNN. Title of the decision

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces and constraints are in play (technical, organisational,
operational)? State the facts, not the solution.

## Decision

What we decided, stated in active voice: "We will ...".

## Consequences

What follows from the decision, both good and bad: what becomes easier, what becomes harder, what
we accept as a trade-off, and any follow-up work it creates.
